Account & Data Deletion (English)

This page explains how to request account and data deletion for UP NUMBER (com.jihun.upnumber) and Summon Merge Defense (com.jihun.summonmergedefense).

Accounts are created with a nickname only — no password, email address, phone number, or OAuth sign-in is used.

How to request deletion: send an email to jihun5968@naver.com with the subject “Account deletion request”, and include the app name and your in-game nickname.

What is deleted: the account ID and authentication token, the nickname, all match records (plays, wins, best score / rating, best wave, rank total) and the account creation timestamp.

Retention: requests are processed within 30 days. Once processed, the data is permanently removed from the server and no long-term backup copies are retained. Data stored on the device is removed when the app is uninstalled.
